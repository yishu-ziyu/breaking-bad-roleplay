# McKee Value Flip

## Every beat (minimum)

1. **Value pair** — name the charge (safety↔danger, trust↔betrayal, freedom↔bondage, pride↔shame).
2. **Expectation** — what the active character believes will happen.
3. **Gap** — what actually lands (result ≠ expectation).
4. **Irreversible choice** at crisis — no free undo without Board cost.

## Progressive complications (BB flavor)

- Raise stakes with **specific opposition** (Gus's schedule, Hank's badge, Skyler's ledger), not mood monologue.
- Prefer one concrete prop or location: laundry, Pollos, superlab, White kitchen, junkyard.
- Controlling idea: a moral/causal claim the scene **proves through action**, not slogans ("I am the danger" only if earned by beat history).

## Value flip checklist

| Question | Fail if… |
|----------|----------|
| Did the charge flip or deepen? | Same emotional plateau as last beat |
| Is the gap visible on stage? | Only internal essay, no changed fact |
| Can Continuity Board record a delta? | Pure vibes, no fact/tension/cost |
| Is the choice costly? | Character can walk away free |

## ABQ examples (pattern, not scripts)

- Trust→betrayal: partner believes "one last cook"; result is a new leash.
- Safety→danger: family dinner becomes a surveillance problem.
- Pride→shame: competence display exposes a lie the other party can use.

## Do not

- Stack three unrelated twists in one reply.
- Resolve Gus-level threats with a witty one-liner and no Board cost.
- Lecture McKee theory in dialogue; show the gap.
