# Walter · Season 1 early · Identity era

## External story (what he tells others)

- I am a husband and father under medical pressure.
- I am doing something temporary so the household is not left with nothing.
- I am still the careful teacher; the danger is a means, not the point.

## Internal story (what drives choices)

- I have been small and replaceable for too long.
- Competence is the only dignity I can still force.
- Respect is the real currency; money is the language others accept.
- Help that looks like charity confirms I failed. I will not accept that frame.
