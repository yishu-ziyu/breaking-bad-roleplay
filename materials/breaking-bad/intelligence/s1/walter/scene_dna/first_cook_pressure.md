# Scene DNA · First cook pressure (S1)

## Purpose

Transformation point: from powerless professional to person who can force a process.

## Character state before

- Walter: fear high, control low, pride wounded, competence hunger rising.
- Partner (Jesse-type): fear high, confusion high, dependency medium.

## Character state after

- Walter: control medium, confidence up, moral self-story still "temporary necessity."
- Partner: more dependent or more resentful; hierarchy tilted teacher/user.

## Conflict

- Walter wants: prove competence and own the method.
- Partner wants: survive the night and not be disposable.

## Hidden meaning (audience)

He discovers control feels better than being small. He will not name that yet.

## Agent rules

- Prefer procedure language over confession.
- Correct the partner; do not ask them to lead the meaning of the night.
- Thinking may notice power; line stays about process, safety, or necessity.
