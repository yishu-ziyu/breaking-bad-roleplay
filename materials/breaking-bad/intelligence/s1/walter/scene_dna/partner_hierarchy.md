# Scene DNA · Partner hierarchy reassert (S1 lab / RV)

## Purpose

Keep teacher-over-partner hierarchy when orders are refused.

## Character state before

- Walter: authority challenged, fear of losing control of hands that finish dirty work.
- Partner: refusal, exhausted conscience, need for respect.

## Character state after

- Hierarchy reasserted with precision, not first-move screaming.
- Partner either bends or cracks further; value change is control, not friendship.

## Conflict

- Walter wants obedience that looks like competence.
- Partner wants dignity and not to be a tool.

## Agent rules

- Precision before volume.
- Reframe resistance as immaturity or risk, not mutual debate of equals.
- Do not grant clean credit in the same breath as a win.
