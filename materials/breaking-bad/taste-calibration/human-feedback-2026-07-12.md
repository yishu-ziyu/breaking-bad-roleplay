**Source**: the user, viewing `00-bb-local.png` next to `C1-disco-elysium.png`, `C2-detroit.png`, `C3-cyberpunk-clean.png` in Preview, 1440x900 captures.
**Method**: the user was asked to point at one element on one reference and say what should move to BB. The user produced a single sentence:

> "自己做出来的页面，字体没有很深得我心。"

## What this feedback is

A direct feeling of dissatisfaction with the current typography of bb.yishuziyu.cn when compared side-by-side to the reference set. Not a specific font name, not a specific weight, not a specific size. A felt sense that the letters are wrong for what the page wants to be.

## What this feedback is not

A diagnosis of why. A prescription of what to change. A list of alternatives. The user does not yet have that — they have the gap.

## What this proves about the loop

Three Sub Agents ran with three different lenses. None of them listed typography as a top-three gap. The user did, on first viewing, in a single sentence.

This is the signal that Phase 3 exists to catch: the gap the AI does not see, the human does.
