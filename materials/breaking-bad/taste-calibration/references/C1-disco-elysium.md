## What is right about it

- Oil-painted illustration dominates two-thirds of the viewport. The product is a character, not a UI.
- Left-side typography reads like a book spine, not a SaaS hero. Strong, narrow, all-caps with serifs.
- Background is painterly chaos in dark teal, orange, and red — feels like standing inside the world, not looking at a screenshot of it.
- Two CTA buttons in muted gold and silver — restrained, almost ceremonial. They sit on the bottom edge like relics on a desk.
- Award row at the bottom is honest and unstyled. Production-grade credibility.

## Axis it represents

**Atmosphere over interface.** The page is a mood, not a menu. You read it like a film poster, not a product page.
